Research

I am a Research Scientist at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and the founder of WillFlow.ai. My work sits where AI meets physical industry: agents that plan and carry out real tasks in factories, and dexterous hands that let robots manipulate the world the way people do.

Industrial AI agents

LLM-based agents that read production and equipment data, plan maintenance and operations tasks, and act through the software a plant already runs, with the reliability and safety checks industry demands. This is what we build at WillFlow.ai.

Agent planningTool useIndustrial workflowsTrustworthy agents

Dexterous hands for embodied AI

Multi-fingered robot hands that grasp and manipulate objects in-hand: learning control policies, using touch as feedback, and bringing skills learned in simulation onto real hardware.

Dexterous manipulationTactile sensingSim-to-real

Earlier work. During my Ph.D. at UMass Lowell with Dr. Yimin Chen, I studied the security of machine learning: backdoor attacks and defenses in federated learning, robust model merging, and privacy risks in RAG and LLM agents. Before that, at SUSTech with Dr. Yinqian Zhang, I worked on blockchain security, including Ethereum private transactions and smart contract vulnerabilities.
